Blender Basics

Blender is a powerful software. While it can do much more, we will be using it for 3D Modelling to get started. Making models of real life things to use in games or other programs.

Meshes

Working with any models in Blender, we call them meshes. By default, you start with a simple mesh of a 3D cube. After you get familiar with moving the mouse and navigating the workspace. Click the cube, press delete on your keyboard, and start fresh.

Adding Meshes

Press A on your keyboard to Add a mesh. A dropdown menu will appear and you can select from a handful of default meshes to add to your program. These meshes have further modifiers in the bottom left corner, such as how many sides the mesh should have.

Keybinds

Blender has so many tools at its' disposal. Being quick with your keybinds is key to making models efficiently. You don't want to waste time scouting all of the tools this program has to offer - just learn the keybinds

Scale Tool

When an object is selected, you can press S on your keyboard to Scale it. You can enter a numeric scale or move your mouse freely to scale it.

Axis

Because this is a 3D modelling software, we have to note our three axis. The X axis, the Y axis, and the Z axis. When using tools, we may want to restrict that tool to a certain axis. Just press the corresponding key on your keyboard to lock to a specific axis. For example, pressing X locks you to the X axis.

Grab tool

When a mesh is selected, press G on your keyboard to use the Grab tool. This allows you to move your mesh, and once again can be moved while restricted to a specific axis.

Joining Meshes

You make need multiple meshes to complete the object you're trying to model. Once you have all of your meshes, hold SHIFT and select all of your meshes. Then press CTRL J to JOIN the meshes into a single mesh

Saving Blender Files

You should create a new folder for these Blender Projects. And maybe even a Unit 01 sub folder. If you are at home, save to wherever you like. If you are a student in my class using a school computer, create your folders and save it to the H: Drive

Key Takeaways

  • Blender can be used to make 3D Models
  • Learn your common keybindings
  • Know how to scale, grab, and lock tools to specific axis
  • Join multiple meshes with the JOIN too by pressing CTRL J
