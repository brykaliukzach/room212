Controls

Controls are the basis of this first unit. Controls are anything from your Toolbox that you add to your program.

Labels

Label controls are how you put text on your form. Labels should be named properly, starting with the lbl prefix and ending with something more specific to what that label is for. For example - lblName or lblHealth.

To change what a label displays, you will need to change its' .Text property. Just type whatever you this label to say in the .Text property.

The .Font property let's you change the font and size of the text.

Pictureboxes

Picturebox controls let you display an image inside of them. Images must be in the .PNG or .JPEG format.

Your pictures may look funny, which is why I recommend using Zoom for the picturebox's Size Mode property.

Picturebox controls should be named with the pic prefix. Just like any control, you name them with the appropriate prefix followed by something specific about that control. For example - picCard or picCharacter.

Buttons

Lastly we have Button controls. Button controls are buttons you can click on to perform some code. We'll talk more about what "clicking" means next lesson.

Button controls should be named with the btn prefix. For example - btnStart or btnFlipCard.

Key Takeaways

  • Controls are anything you add to your form from the Toolbox.
  • Each control has a specific prefix you should use when naming it.
  • Control's have properties you can change to customize them
