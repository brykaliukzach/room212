The .Image & .Text Properties

Now that we know the process for changing a control's properties with code, let's take a look at two common properties we will change - a Picturebox's .Image property, and a label's .Text property

Project's Resources

To change a picturebox's Image, we need to first import all the images we may need into our program. This can be done by dragging your images into your project's resources. Found by going to Project -> Properties -> Resources

Once you have all of your images imported into your project, we can change which image a picturebox is showing with code.

picMain.Image = Properties.Resources.name_of_your_image;

As you can see, we're changing the .Image property of our picturebox control. What are we changing it to? Well we're changing it to whatever image we want that's been imported into our resources. So to go there, we set it equal to = Properties.Resouces.name_of_image;

Changing Text

To change the text of a label (or any other control), it's more straight forward

lblMain.Text = "I am changing the text to this!";

Here, we are changing the .Text of a label control. The one thing to watch out for is the text your setting it equal to must be inside a pair of double quotations " ".

Key Takeaways

  • Your images can be dropped into your Project's Resources
  • Change a picturebox's image by setting it equal to an image in your resources.
  • Change the text property by setting it equal to words between double quotations
